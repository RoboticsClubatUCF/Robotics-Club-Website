Welcome to the Robotics Club of Central Florida (RCCF). To ensure a safe, productive, and collaborative environment for all members, we require both new and returning members to read and agree to the following guidelines and responsibilities.

1. Club Constitution and Bylaws
I acknowledge that I have received access to, read, and understand the official RCCF Constitution and Bylaws. I agree to abide by the rules, regulations, and operational procedures outlined within these
documents while participating in club activities, meetings, and official communications via club domains and servers.

2. Safety and Equipment Usage
I will adhere to all safety protocols when working in the lab, workshop, or testing environments. I agree to use club equipment—including but not limited to 3D printers, fabrication tools, test benches, microcontrollers, and battery systems—only after receiving proper training and explicit
authorization. I will report any damaged equipment, software/server infrastructure issues, safety hazards, or injuries to the club leadership immediately.

3. Professional Conduct and Respect
I will treat all fellow members, club leadership, university faculty, and external partners with respect and professionalism.
I will foster an inclusive environment and will not engage in harassment, discrimination, or any behavior that compromises the collaborative nature of the club. I understand that I represent RCCF and the University of Central Florida during off-campus events, major competitions, and online interactions, and I will conduct myself accordingly.

4. Commitment and Participation
I understand that active participation is vital to the success of our technical projects and design proposals. I commit to attending scheduled team meetings, contributing to my assigned tasks, maintaining team documentation, and communicating proactively with project leads if I am unable to meet my commitments.

5. Financial Responsibilities
I agree to pay any applicable club dues in a timely manner. If I am authorized to make purchases on behalf of the club for parts or materials, I will strictly follow the RCCF reimbursement procedures, ensuring all required documentation and receipts are submitted to the leadership team for approval.
